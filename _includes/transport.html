Resbaz will be at UniSC Moreton Bay campus, in Petrie. (See UniSC PDF maps for this campus.)

Getting to campus

UniSC Moreton Bay is accessible by public transport and cycling (see a map of cycle paths on OSM). We encourage to opt for one of these more sustainable options, but if you need to drive,see the important information about parking below.

Public transport

Public transport is currently 50 cents per trip, making it a lot more affordable than driving! The most convenient public transport stop to get to ResBaz is the Petrie train station, 400 m from the campus. Plan your public transport journey with Translink's Journey Planner. From the centre of Brisbane, this is a direct trip taking between 30 and 40 minutes.

More information about transport can be found on the UniSC website.

Parking

Note that on-campus parking is normally only available to UniSC card holders, but for the duration of the conference, parking P1 (next to the conference building A1) will be accessible to conference attendees without swipe access.

Please proceed through the A1 parking boom gate and park in any available space. In the event that the boom gate does not open, please use the call button and advise security that you are attending the Research bazaar to gain access.

Please note that under no circumstances can visitors park in the Queensland Rail carpark. This is for Queensland Rail commuters only and is monitored. Parking in this carpark may result in fines.

On campus

Registration happens outside the keynote auditoria A1.G.23/25 (Building A1, ground floor, Rooms 23/25). Wayfinding signs will be placed around building A1 to easily find the rooms. For more information about accessibility, please see the campus accessibility map.
