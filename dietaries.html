While we endeavour to meet all dietary needs, we would like to be open with what we're offering to ensure you can make your best decisions regarding any diet, cultural or personal needs.



Morning tea



We will provide a selection of light morning refreshments, including fruit, muesli bars, and assorted snacks. For those with larger appetites, we recommend bringing additional food.
Tea and coffee will not be provided, so please bring your own thermos, or order from the local coffee outlets. There are numerous water refill stations on campus. We encourage you to bring a reusable water bottle and coffee cup to support our sustainability initiatives.



Lunch

For lunch, we will be supplying wraps and sandwiches, including vegetarian and gluten free.

For those where this may not fit your dietary needs, we suggest bringing in your lunch. There are student kitchens on campus to reheat your meal. Food purchasing options are also available as below.
